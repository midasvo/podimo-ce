+ Using Apple, Facebook, or Google to log in to Podimo?

Try the following steps:

  1. Log in at Podimo like you normally would.
  2. Find the email address associated with your account. For Apple, this looks something like abcdefg@privaterelay.appleid.com. You should be able to find it somewhere on the settings page.
  3. Log out of Podimo.
  4. Instead of logging in to Podimo like normal, use the “Forgot password” form to reset your password. Put the email address from step 2 in here.
  5. Reset your password with the link from your mailbox.
  6. You should now be able to log in to Podimo with the email address from step 2 and the password you just created. You don’t have to use the Sign in with Apple / Facebook / Google buttons anymore.
  7. Verify that you can log in to Podimo with your new credentials. If you can, then you are able to use this site!
